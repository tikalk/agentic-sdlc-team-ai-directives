# CDR-006: Accepted-only quality gate before artifact generation

## Context

Multi-stage decision lifecycles (draft → clarify → implement) break down when the generation stage consumes unreviewed inputs — the output bakes in undecided trade-offs that later require regeneration.

## Decision

The generation/implementation stage processes only records with Accepted status and skips all others. Approval lives in the clarify stage; generation never approves implicitly.

## Rejected Alternatives

* Generate from all records regardless of status — bakes undecided trade-offs into artifacts
* Implicit approval by generation — destroys the review trail

## Reason

Status-gating makes "accepted" mean exactly one thing — safe for downstream compilation — and lets clarify and implement evolve independently without silent coupling.

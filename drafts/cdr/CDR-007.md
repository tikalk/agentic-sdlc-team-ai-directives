# CDR-007: Binary pass/fail evaluation criteria only

## Context

Likert-scale or graded eval criteria produce ambiguous verdicts — a "3/5" tells neither the grader nor the fixer what to do, and grader agreement collapses on middle scores.

## Decision

Define every evaluation criterion with strict binary pass/fail conditions only, each paired with balanced real pass/fail examples plus generated adversarial edge cases. No graded scales.

## Rejected Alternatives

* Likert/graded scales — ambiguous middle scores, poor grader agreement
* Pass conditions without examples — uncalibrated, drift between grader runs

## Reason

Binary verdicts are actionable (fix or don't) and measurable (agreement rate, TPR/TNR). Balanced plus adversarial examples pin the decision boundary where prose alone cannot.

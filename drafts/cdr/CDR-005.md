# CDR-005: Confidence scoring (base + evidence bonus × usage) with tier-gated review

## Context

Not all mined directive candidates deserve equal review effort. Treating every candidate identically either over-reviews strong candidates or under-reviews weak ones.

## Decision

Score each candidate as type base score plus additive evidence bonuses, multiplied by a usage/success multiplier, then route by threshold tiers (HIGH auto-accept after review, MEDIUM deliberate review, LOW keep as draft). Calibrate numeric weights per team; keep the formula shape fixed.

## Rejected Alternatives

* Uniform review for all candidates — wastes effort on strong ones, risks weak ones
* Purely subjective triage — unrepeatable, uncalibratable across sessions

## Reason

The formula separates what is team-tunable (weights, thresholds) from what is structural (evidence must raise confidence, proven reuse must compound it), so scoring improves with data instead of opinion.
